# Open Texts - Index

## What this is

There are a few pieces of text that I think are important to a wide section of society but are not available in the most easily consumable form. This is a personal effort to give these texts the digital editions they deserve.

If a text is in the public domain and its best available form is a (/blurry) scan or an inaccessible regional language edition, it belongs here. Every text has been manually digitized, structured, and in many cases translated by Sourav Kulkarni (with the help of AI tools).

The list of texts is completely subjective. If you think some texts are worth adding, please send an email to `srvklkrn@gmail.com`. If you want to contribute, please make a pull request to the repo @Sourav-Kulkarni/open-texts

---

## How texts are organized

Texts are organized by author. Where authorship is unknown or multiple, they are organized by work title.

```
savarkar/
    sanyasta-khadga.md
aryabhata/
    aryabhatiya.md
arthashastra/
    arthashastra.md
```
