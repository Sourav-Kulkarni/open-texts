## १ - १०

गणाधीश जो ईश सर्वां गुणांचा।<br>
मुळारंभ आरंभ तो निर्गुणाचा॥<br>
नमूं शारदा मूळ चत्वार वाचा।<br>
गमूं पंथ आनंत या राघवाचा॥१॥

**Verse 1**: God Ganesh, the god of all virtues and to whom the spirit owes its origin; Goddess Sharda, the goddess of speech; we bow to them. Now we will explore the way to reach Lord Rama which is considered as infinite.

मना सज्जना भक्तिपंथेचि जावें।<br>
तरी श्रीहरी पाविजेतो स्वभावें॥<br>
जनीं निंद्य तें सर्व सोडूनि द्यावें।<br>
जनीं वंद्य ते सर्व भावे करावे॥२॥

**Verse 2**: Let us follow the path of honesty and devotion. This path will lead us to Shri Hari. Let us shun everything that is condemned and do everything that is commendable with the full devotion of mind.

प्रभाते मनी राम चिंतीत जावा।<br>
पुढे वैखरी राम आधी वदावा॥<br>
सदाचार हा थोर सांडूं नये तो।<br>
जनीं तोचि तो मानवी धन्य होतो॥३॥

**Verse 3**: Let us remember Lord Rama every morning. Let us start all our work by chanting Shri Ram. Let us always stick to the good behaviour. Only the person of good action is held in esteem in this world.

मना वासना दुष्ट कामा न ये रे।<br>
मना सर्वथा पापबुद्धी नको रे॥<br>
मना धर्मता नीति सोडूं नको हो।<br>
मना अंतरीं सार वीचार राहो॥४॥

**Verse 4**: Our evil desires are of no use. Sinful thoughts should not come to our mind. We should always have ethics. We should always know the real truth in our mind.

मना पापसंकल्प सोडूनि द्यावा।<br>
मना सत्यसंकल्प जीवीं धरावा॥<br>
मना कल्पना ते नको वीषयांची।<br>
विकारे घडे हो जनी सर्व ची ची॥५॥

**Verse 5**: Let us shun all sinful plans. Let us work on the plan that will lead us to the truth. Let us not always think about sensual pleasures. If our mind gets corrupted then we lose our reputation amidst people.

नको रे मना क्रोध हा खेदकारी।<br>
नको रे मना काम नाना विकारी॥<br>
नको रे मना लोभ हा अंगिकारू।<br>
नको रे मना मत्सरु दंभ भारु॥६॥

**Verse 6**: Let us say no to anger which makes us feel sorry. Let us not corrupt our mind with all sorts of desires. Let us shun the doership of actions. Let us not praise ourselves due to the jealousy for others.

मना श्रेष्ठ धारिष्ट जीवीं धरावे।<br>
मना बोलणे नीच सोशीत जावें॥<br>
स्वयें सर्वदा नम्र वाचे वदावे।<br>
मना सर्व लोकांसि रे नीववावें॥७॥

**Verse 7**: Let us have great patience. Let us bear the evil words spoken to us. Let us always speak politely. Let us always understand others.

देहे त्यागितां कीर्ति मागें उरावी।<br>
मना सज्जना हेचि क्रीया धरावी॥<br>
मना चंदनाचे परी त्वां झिजावे।<br>
परी अंतरीं सज्जना नीववावे॥८॥

**Verse 8**: Let us do something so that our good name remains even after perishing of our body. Let us do all good actions honestly. Let us wear out like sandalwood. Let others receive benefit out of our sufferings.

नको रे मना द्रव्य ते पूढिलांचे।<br>
अति स्वार्थबुद्धी नुरे पाप सांचे॥<br>
घडे भोगणे पाप ते कर्म खोटे।<br>
न होतां मनासारिखें दु:ख मोठे॥९॥

**Verse 9**: Let us not keep an eye on others money. Selfishness adds to our sins. Let us avoid actions that will lead to incurring sin. We will always feel sad if we have lot of expectations.

सदा सर्वदा प्रीती रामीं धरावी।<br>
दुःखाची स्वयें सांडि जीवी करावी॥<br>
देहेदु:ख ते सूख मानीत जावे।<br>
विवेके सदा स्वस्वरुपीं भरावें॥१०॥

**Verse 10**: Let us always have love for Rama. Let us remove sadness from mind. We should learn to consider the sufferings that our body undergoes for good cause as happiness. We should always have knowledge of our true self in our mind.

## ११ - २०

जनीं सर्वसूखी असा कोण आहे।<br>
विचारें मना तुंचि शोधुनि पाहे॥<br>
मना त्वांचि रे पूर्वसंचीत केले।<br>
तयासारिखे भोगणें प्राप्त जाले ॥११॥

**Verse 11**: Who in this world is happy in all respects. Let us think over it. Whatever we have accumulated through our past actions, we have to suffer on that account.

मना मानसीं दु:ख आणूं नको रे।<br>
मना सर्वथा शोक चिंता नको रे॥<br>
विवेके देहेबुद्धि सोडूनि द्यावी।<br>
विदेहीपणें मुक्ति भोगीत जावी॥१२॥

**Verse 12**: We should not bleed our mind with sorrow. We should not be engrossed with sadness and worries. We should discover our true self and should understand that our existence is not restricted to our body alone. Our existence is beyond our body and free from all shackles.

मना सांग पां रावणा काय जाले।<br>
अकस्मात ते राज्य सर्वै बुडाले॥<br>
म्हणोनी कुडी वासना सांड वेगीं।<br>
बळे लागला काळ हा पाठिलागी॥१३॥

**Verse 13**: See what happened to Ravana. He lost the whole kingdom all of a sudden. Therefore let us shun evil desires. Let us know that the death will consume us in the course of time.

जिवा कर्मयोगे जनीं जन्म जाला।<br>
परी शेवटीं काळमूखीं निमाला॥<br>
महाथोर ते मृत्युपंथेचि गेले।<br>
कितीएक ते जन्मले आणि मेले॥१४॥

**Verse 14**: All beings of this world take birth and lose life. Even the greatest ones cannot escape from death. Many have born and died.

मना पाहतां सत्य हे मृत्युभूमी।<br>
जितां बोलती सर्वही जीव मी मी॥<br>
चिरंजीव हे सर्वही मानिताती।<br>
अकस्मात सांडूनिया सर्व जाती॥१५॥

**Verse 15**: If seen properly we can see that everyone on this earth has to die. But still people magnify their ego. People think that they wont die and they will live forever. But all of a sudden everything is gone.

मरे एक त्याचा दुजा शोक वाहे।<br>
अकस्मात तोही पुढे जात आहे॥<br>
पुरेना जनीं लोभ रे क्षोभ त्याते।<br>
म्हणोनी जनीं मागुता जन्म घेते॥१६॥

**Verse 16**: People feel sad about death of their dear ones. They are themselves heading to death. Greed makes one angry. Greed also pushes one into the cycle of death and rebirth.

मनीं मानवा व्यर्थ चिंता वहाते।<br>
अकस्मात होणार होऊनि जाते॥<br>
घडें भोगणे सर्वही कर्मयोगे।<br>
मतीमंद तें खेद मानी वियोगें॥१७॥

**Verse 17**: People keep their minds full of sorrows and worries. But the thing, that has to happen, happens suddenly. We should renounce the doership in everything that happens. A person who can not understand feels sad about the happenings.

मना राघवेंवीण आशा नको रे।<br>
मना मानवाची नको कीर्ति तूं रे॥<br>
जया वर्णिती वेद-शास्त्रे-पुराणें।<br>
तया वर्णितां सर्वही श्लाघ्यवाणे॥१८॥

**Verse 18**: We should always long for Lord Rama. We should not magnify our ego. Lord Rama is described by the Vedas and the Puranas. After describing Lord Rama nothing more needs to be described.

मना सर्वथा सत्य सांडूं नको रे।<br>
मना सर्वथा मिथ्य मांडूं नको रे॥<br>
मना सत्य ते सत्य वाचे वदावे।<br>
मना मिथ्य तें मिथ्य सोडूनि द्यावें॥१९॥

**Verse 19**: We should never be away from the truth. We should not concieve anything without base. We should speak the truth and shun the baseless.

बहू हिंपुटी होईजे मायपोटी।<br>
नको रे मना यातना तेचि मोठी॥<br>
निरोधें पचे कोंडिले गर्भवासी।<br>
अधोमूख रे दु:ख त्या बाळकासीं॥२०॥

**Verse 20**: If we are trapped in the cycle of death and rebirth then we have to undergo great hardships.

## २१ - ३०

मना वासना चूकवीं येरझारा।<br>
मना कामना सांडी रे द्रव्यदारा॥<br>
मना यातना थोर हे गर्भवासीं।<br>
मना सज्जना भेटवीं राघवासीं॥२१॥

**Verse 21**: Let us keep the desires at a distance. Let us not disturb the tranquility of mind. Let us renounce the cravings for wealth and wife. Constantly changing the state of mind results in agony. Let us reach out to Lord Rama by adhering to the path he has shown.

मना सज्जना हीत माझें करावें।<br>
रघुनायका दृढ चित्ती धरावें॥<br>
महाराज तो स्वामि वायुसुताचा।<br>
जना उद्धरी नाथ लोकत्रयाचा॥२२॥

**Verse 22**: Let us do good to ourselves. Let us install firmly personality of Lord Rama in the mind. Lord Rama is the master of Hanuman, the son of the Wind God. He can grant salvation to all the three worlds.

न बोलें मना राघवेवीण कांहीं।<br>
जनी वाउगें बोलता सुख नाहीं॥<br>
घडिने घडी काळ आयुष्य नेतो।<br>
देहांतीं तुला कोण सोडूं पहातो?॥२३॥

**Verse 23**: Let us speak the truth. Speaking other than truth will not lead to happiness. The life is ending continuously by the passing of time. What can we do once the life is over?

रघुनायकावीण वांया शिणावे।<br>
जनासारिखे व्यर्थ कां वोसणावें॥<br>
सदा सर्वदा नाम वाचे वसो दे।<br>
अहंता मनी पापिणी ते नसो दे॥२४॥

**Verse 24**: Let us not put ourselves to trouble by forgetting Lord Rama. Let us not speak rubbish. Let us speak the truth. Let the sin in the form of ego be kept away from our mind.

मना वीट मानूं नको बोलण्याचा।<br>
पुढें मागुता राम जोडेल कैंचा॥<br>
सुखाची घडी लोटतां सूख आहे।<br>
पुढें सर्व जाईल कांही न राहे॥२५॥

**Verse 25**: Dont get fed up by speaking the truth. Otherwise how You can unite with Lord Rama? You want to enjoy the present moment. Think what remains thereafter.

देहेरक्षणाकारणें यत्न केला।<br>
परी शेवटीं काळ घेउन गेला॥<br>
करीं रे मना भक्ति या राघवाची।<br>
पुढें अंतरीं सोडिं चिंता भवाची॥२६॥

**Verse 26**: You dont want to undertake any work. You want to give rest to your body. Ultimately the body will come to an end by the passing of time. Show the courage to stick to the qualities on the basis of which Lord Rama lived his life. Dont burn your heart for the wordly worries.

भवाच्या भये काय भीतोस लंडी।<br>
धरीं रे मना धीर धाकासि सांडी॥<br>
रघूनायकासारिखा स्वामि शीरीं।<br>
नुपेक्षी कदा कोपल्या दंडधारी॥२७॥

**Verse 27**: Dont get let down by the wordly miseries. Show courage. Give up the mental stress. Lord Rama is the protector. He wont forget you even if the whole world goes adverse to you.

दिनानाथ हा राम कोदंडधारी।<br>
पुढें देखतां काळ पोटीं थरारी॥<br>
मना वाक्य नेमस्त हे सत्य मानीं।<br>
नुपेक्षी कदा राम दासाभिमानी॥२८॥

**Verse 28**: Lord Rama, who is the protector of the afflicted, welds a bow in his hand. Even the death shivers at the sight of Lord Rama. Let us understand the truth.

पदी राघवाचे सदा ब्रीद गाजे।<br>
वळें भक्तरीपूशिरी कांबि वाजे॥<br>
पुरी वाहिली सर्व जेणे विमानीं।<br>
नुपेक्षी कदा रामदासाभिमानी॥२९॥

**Verse 29**: Lord Rama protects them who abide by his qualities.

समर्थाचिया सेचका वक्र पाहे।<br>
असा सर्व भुमंडळी कोण आहे॥<br>
जयाची लिला वर्णिती लोक तीन्ही।<br>
नुपेक्षी कदा राम दासाभिमानी॥३०॥

**Verse 30**: He who works for the noble cause need not worry about the enemies in the world.

## ३१ - ४०

महासंकटी सोडिले देव जेणें।<br>
प्रतापे बळे आगळा सर्वगूणे॥<br>
जयाते स्मरे शैलजा शूलपाणी।<br>
नुपेक्षी कदा राम दासाभिमानी॥३१॥

**Verse 31**: On the basis of wisdom, organisation skills and resource base in the form of armoury etc. Lord Rama released Gods from the prison of Ravana. Lord Rama is fondly remembered by Shiv and Parvati.

अहल्या शिळा राघवें मुक्त केली।<br>
पदीं लागतां दिव्य होऊनि गेली॥<br>
जया वर्णितां शीणली वेदवाणी।<br>
नुपेक्षी कदा राम दासाभिमानी॥३२॥

**Verse 32**: The touch of Rama's feet to a rock brought back to life the wife of Rishi Gautam. Even the Vedas are not able to comprehend him.

वसे मेरुमांदार हे सृष्टिलीला ।<br>
शशी सूर्य तारांगणे मेघमाला॥<br>
चिरंजीव केले जनी दास दोन्ही।<br>
नुपेक्षी कदा राम दासाभिमानी॥३३॥

**Verse 33**: The Laws of Nature and the objects of Nature find their support in Lord Rama. He granted boon of immortality to Hanuman and Bhibhishana.

उपेक्षी कदा रामरुपी असेना।<br>
जिवां मानवां निश्चयो तो वसेना॥<br>
शिरी भार वाहेन बोले पुराणीं।<br>
नुपेक्षी कदा राम दासाभिमानी॥३४॥

**Verse 34**: Never disregard Lord Rama. It is very difficult for human-beings to abide by his qualities. Wonderful are the results for those who abide by his qualities. The Puranas stand testimony to this fact.

असे हो जया अंतरी भाव जैसा।<br>
वसे हो तया अंतरी देव तैसा॥<br>
अनन्यास रक्षीतसे चापपाणी।<br>
नुपेक्षी कदा राम दासाभिमानी॥३५॥

**Verse 35**: You see God in the same form as that you have in your mind. Those who are fully devoted to Lord Rama are protected by him.

सदा सर्वदा देव सन्नीध आहे।<br>
कृपाळुपणे अल्प धारीष्ट पाहे॥<br>
सुखानंद आनंद कैवल्यदानी।<br>
नुपेक्षी कदा राम दासाभिमानी॥३६॥

**Verse 36**: God is always looking at you. Have the courage and see him. He will give you happiness and salvation.

सदा चक्रवाकासि मार्तंड जैसा।<br>
उडी घालितो संकटी स्वामि तैसा॥<br>
हरीभक्तिचा घाव गाजे निशाणी।<br>
नुपेक्षी कदा राम दासाभिमानी॥३७॥

**Verse 37**: The darkness cannot sustain after rising of Sun. Similarly wordly troubles disappear after seeing Lord Rama.

मना प्रार्थना तूजला एक आहे।<br>
रघूराज थक्कीत होऊनि पाहे॥<br>
अवज्ञा कदा हो यदर्थी न कीजे।<br>
मना सज्जना राघवी वस्ति कीजे॥३८॥

**Verse 38**: Let us pray to our mind to always remain devoted to Lord Rama.

जया वर्णिती वेद शास्त्रे पुराणे।<br>
जयाचेनि योगें समाधान बाणे॥<br>
तयालागिं हें सर्व चांचल्य दीजे।<br>
मना सज्जना राघवी वस्ति कीजे॥३९॥

**Verse 39**: Lord Rama is the subject matter of Vedas and Puranas. He is the objective of the penance of Yogis. Give to Him all uneasiness of your mind. Let your mind always dwell on Lord Rama.

मना पाविजे सर्वही सूख जेथे।<br>
अति आदरें ठेविजे लक्ष तेथें॥<br>
विविकें कुडी कल्पना पालटिजे।<br>
मना सज्जना राघवी वस्ति कीजे॥४०॥

**Verse 40**: Your mind is entangled in the false imaginations. Cause a change in the state of your mind through the power of discrimination.

## ४१ - ५०

बहू हिंडतां सौख्य होणार नाहीं।<br>
शिणावे परी नातुडे हीत कांहीं॥<br>
विचारें बरें अंतरा बोधवीजे।<br>
मना सज्जना राघवीं वस्ति कीजे॥४१॥<br>

**Verse 41**: Dont wander your mind too much. There wont be any happiness after that. The mind will get tired. Let us understand at the level of our conscious that our mind should rest at the feet of Lord Rama.

बहुतांपरी हेंचि आतां धरावें।<br>
रघूनायका आपुलेसे करावें॥<br>
दिनानाथ हें तोडरीं ब्रीद गाजे।<br>
मना सज्जना राघवीं वस्ति कीजे॥४२॥<br>

**Verse 42**: After having said many times, listen again that do everything so that Lord Rama will give you place at his feet.

मना सज्जना एक जीवीं धरावें।<br>
जनी आपुलें हीत तूवां करावें॥<br>
रघूनायकावीण बोलो नको हो।<br>
सदा मानसीं तो निजध्यास राहो॥४३॥<br>

**Verse 43**: Hold it firmly in your mind. Let us do good to us. While speaking let our mind dwell on the truth. Let our mind always dwell on Lord Rama.

मना रे जनीं मौनमुद्रा धरावी।<br>
कथा आदरे राघवाची करावी॥<br>
नसें राम ते धाम सोडूनि द्यावे।<br>
सुखालागिं आरण्य सेवीत जावे॥४४॥<br>

**Verse 44**: When we are amidst people, we should keep quiet. We should always tell stories glorifying Lord Rama. If at any place people are not taking interest in Lord Rama then we should leave that place.

जयाचेनि संगे समाधान भंगे।<br>
अहंता अकस्मात येऊनि लागे॥<br>
तये संगतीची जनीं कोण गोडी।<br>
जिये संगतीनें मती राम सोडी॥४५॥<br>

**Verse 45**: We should learn to undergo hardships for the sake of happiness.

मना जे घडी राघवेवीण गेली।<br>
जनीं आपुली ते तुवां हानि केली॥<br>
रघूनायकावीण तो शीण आहे।<br>
जनी दक्ष तो लक्ष लावूनि पाहे॥४६॥<br>

**Verse 46**: The persons whose company make us to lose balance of our mind,The company of persons that raises our ego, Let there be no such company that detach us from Lord Rama. The moment that is gone without Lord Rama is a loss. Such moments devoid of Lord Rama make us feel tired. Let us always be focused and alert.

मनीं लोचनीं श्रीहरी तोचि पाहे।<br>
जनीं जाणतां मुक्त होऊनि राहे॥<br>
गुणीं प्रीति राखे क्रमू साधनाचा।<br>
जगीं धन्य तो दास सर्वोत्तमाचा॥४७॥<br>

**Verse 47**: Our eyes should see the truth. Let us be alert and devoted while working with the people. We should build the resource base for doing work of public good. We should do our work systematically and sequentially. He who works like this is worth for living.

सदा देवकाजीं झिजे देह ज्याचा।<br>
सदा रामनामें वदे नित्य साचा॥<br>
स्वधर्मेचि चाले सदा उत्तमाचा।<br>
जगीं धन्य तो दास सर्वोत्तमाचा॥४८॥<br>

**Verse 48**: Our body should wear out for the cause of others. We should speak the truth. In whatever situation we are in, we should deal in the best interest of everyone concerned.

सदा बोलण्यासारिखे चालताहे।<br>
अनेकीं सदा एक देवासि पाहे॥<br>
सगूणी भजे लेश नाही भ्रमाचा।<br>
जगीं धन्य तो दास सर्वोत्तमाचा॥४९॥<br>

**Verse 49**: We should implement what we say. Let us understand the real truth out of its different shades. Let us always abide by the good qualities and let us not be confused.

नसे अंतरी काम नानाविकारी।<br>
उदासीन जो तापसी ब्रह्मचारी॥<br>
निवाला मनीं लेश नाही तमाचा।<br>
जगी धन्य तो दास सर्वोत्तमाचा॥५०॥<br>

**Verse 50**: Our mind should not be full of desires and weaknesses. Let our mind undertake the penance. Let us be aloof from whatever conditions arise. Let us understand the all-pervading Brahma present everywhere. Let us purify our mind. Let all darkness be dispelled.

## ५१ - ६०

मदें मत्सरें सांडिली स्वार्थबुद्धी।<br>
प्रपंचीक नाहीं जयातें उपाधी॥<br>
सदा बोलणे नम्र वाचा सुवाचा।<br>
जगी धन्य तो दास सर्वोत्तमाचा॥५१॥

**Verse 51**: A mind that is intoxicated and jealous cannot understand the good course of action. Therefore let us show the exit route to intoxication, jealousy and selfishness. Let us speak politely.

क्रमी वेळ जो तत्त्वचिंतानुवादे।<br>
न लिंपे कदा दंभ वादे विवादे॥<br>
करी सुखसंवाद जो उगमाचा।<br>
जगी धन्य तो दास सर्वोत्तमाचा॥५२॥

**Verse 52**: Let us spend time for understanding the truth. Let us not indulge in discussions and debates to prove our intelligence. Let us make others happy and try to realise the all-pervading Brahma.

सदा आर्जवी प्रीय जो सर्व लोकीं।<br>
सदा सर्वदा सत्यवादी विवेकी॥<br>
न बोले कदा मिथ्य वाचा त्रिवाचा।<br>
जगी धन्य तो दास सर्वोत्तमाचा॥५३॥

**Verse 53**: Let us be polite to all. Let our behaviour be such that we are liked by all. Let us speak the truth and let us have a sound power of discrimination. Let us not speak rubbish.

सदा सेवि आरण्य तारुण्यकाळीं।<br>
मिळेना कदा कल्पनेचेनि मेळी॥<br>
चळेना मनीं निश्चयो दृढ ज्याचा।<br>
जगीं धन्य तो दास सर्वोत्तमाचा॥५४॥

**Verse 54**: Let us undertake hardships when our body is strong. Let us not be carried away with the wild imaginations. Let the determination of our mind be strong.

नसे मानसीं नष्ट आशा दुराशा।<br>
वसे अंतरीं प्रेमपाशा पिपाशा॥<br>
ऋणी देव हा भक्तिभावे जयाचा।<br>
जगीं धन्य तो दास सर्वोत्तमाचा॥५५॥

**Verse 55**: Let there be no useless and ugly cravings in our mind. Let our mind be full of love. God is kind to become indebted to his devotees for their devotion to him.

दिनाचा दयाळू मनाचा मवाळू।<br>
स्नेहाळू कृपाळू जनीं दासपाळू॥<br>
तया अंतरी क्रोध संताप कैंचा।<br>
जगीं धन्य तो दास सर्वोत्तमाचा॥५६॥

**Verse 56**: Let us be kind to the afflicted. Let our mind have love and care for others. Let our mind be free from anger and irritation.

जगीं होइजे धन्य या रामनामे।<br>
क्रिया भक्ति ऊपासना नित्य नेमे॥<br>
उदासीनता तत्त्वता सार आहे।<br>
सदा सर्वदा मोकळी वृत्ति राहे ॥५७॥

**Verse 57**: Let us meditate on the truth to justify our existence. Let us always be devoted to the good work. Let us treat others at par with us i.e. let us understand that we are also governed by the same laws of nature as others are. Let us keep our mind free from impurities.

नको वासना वीषयीं वृत्तिरुपें।<br>
पदार्थी जडे कामना पूर्वपापें॥<br>
सदा राम निष्काम चिंतीत जावा।<br>
मना कल्पनालेश तोहि नसावा॥५८॥

**Verse 58**: Let our mind not crave for sensual enjoyments. Let us understand that craving for sensual pleasures is due to the sins commited in the past. Let our mind always dwell on Lord Rama. Let us not indulge in futile imaginations even for a while.

मना कल्पना कल्पितां कल्पकोटी।<br>
नव्हे रे नव्हे सर्वथा रामभेटी॥<br>
मनीं कामना राम नाही जयाला।<br>
अती आदरे प्रीती नाही तयाला॥५९॥

**Verse 59**: Our imaginations have no end. But our imaginations will not lead us to the truth. If our mind is not dwelling on Lord Rama, how can we have love and respect for him?

मना राम कल्पतरु कामधेनु।<br>
निधी सार चिंतामणी काय वानूं॥<br>
जयाचेनि योगे घडे सर्व सत्ता।<br>
तया साम्यता कायसी कोण आतां॥६०॥

**Verse 60**: Lord Rama is the wish-yielding tree. He is the wish-yielding cow. He can relieve us of all our sorrows. He symbolises the truth. Let us understand that the world rests on him. None is comparable to Lord Rama.

## ६१ - ७०

उभा कल्पवृक्षातळीं दु:ख वाहे।<br>
तया अंतरीं सर्वदा तेचि आहे॥<br>
जनी सज्जनी वाद हा वाढवावा।<br>
पुढें मागता शोक जीवीं धरावा॥६१॥

**Verse 61**: You are standing under the wish-yielding tree and you are sad. Let us realise Lord Rama who is residing within us. Let us spread this theory of 'Ram is everywhere' amidst people. If we dont do this then we will feel sad in the future.

निजध्यास तो सर्व तुटोनि गेला।<br>
बळें अंतरीं शोक संताप ठेला॥<br>
सुखानंद आनंद भेदें बुडाला।<br>
मना निश्चयो सर्व खेदे उडाला॥६२॥

**Verse 62**: We have forgotten our true self. We are intoxicated and therefore we feel sad. We are confused between happiness and sensual pleasures. We are so sad that we are losing determination of our mind.

घरी कामधेनू पुढें ताक मागें।<br>
हरीबोध सांडोनि वेवाद लागे॥<br>
करी सार चिंतामणी काचखंडे।<br>
तया मागतां देत आहे उदंडे॥६३॥

**Verse 63**: We have a wish-yielding cow at our home and we are begging for butter-milk. Our mind is not dwelling on Lord Rama. Our mind is busy in raking up debates. Let us not break a ' sorrow removing gem ' for the sake of many pieces of glass.

अती मूढ त्या दृढ बुद्धि असेना।<br>
अती काम त्या राम चित्ती वसेना॥<br>
अती लोभ त्या क्षोभ होइल जाणा।<br>
अती वीषयी सर्वदा दैन्यवाणा॥६४॥

**Verse 64**: A foolish person cannot have any power of discrimination. He who is always after sensual pleasures cannot hold Lord Rama in his heart. He who is greedy is bound to become sad. The one who is always after sensual pleasures is useless.

नको दैन्यवाणें जिणे भक्तिऊणे।<br>
अती मुर्ख त्या सर्वदा दु:ख दूणे॥<br>
धरीं रे मना आदरें प्रीति रामी।<br>
नको वासना हेमधामीं विरामीं॥६५॥

**Verse 65**: Let us say no to the worthless existence devoid of devotion. He who is a fool is always two times sad. Let our mind have love and respect at the feet of Lord Rama. Let us not long for money always.

नव्हे सार संसार हा घोर आहे।<br>
मना सज्जना सत्य शोधुनि पाहे॥<br>
जनीं वीष खातां पुढे सूख कैचे।<br>
करीं रे मना ध्यान या राघवाचें॥६६॥

**Verse 66**: Many things keep on happening around us. Let us try to find out the substance of such happenings. If our mind is made impure then we will not become peaceful and happy. Let us meditate on Lord Rama.

घनश्याम हा राम लावण्यरुपी।<br>
महाधीर गंभीर पूर्णप्रतापी॥<br>
करी संकटीं सेवकांचा कुडावा।<br>
प्रभाते मनी राम चिंतीत जावा॥६७॥

**Verse 67**: Lord Rama is the ocean of beauty and good qualities. He has a great courage. He is serious in his work. His performance is great. He protects his devotees in their distress. Let us remember Lord Rama every morning.

बळें आगळा राम कोदंडधारी।<br>
महाकाळ विक्राळ तोही थरारी॥<br>
पुढे मानवा किंकरा कोण केवा।<br>
प्रभाते मनी राम चिंतीत जावा॥६८॥

**Verse 68**: Lord Rama who is welding a bow in hand is very powerful. Even the terrible death shivers at his sight. We are petty in all regards. Let us remember Lord Rama every morning.

सुखानंदकारी निवारी भयातें।<br>
जनीं भक्तिभावे भजावे तयातें॥<br>
विवेके त्यजावा अनाचार हेवा।<br>
प्रभाते मनी राम चिंतीत जावा॥६९॥

**Verse 69**: Lord Rama creates bliss and relieves sorrows. Let us join others for collective devotion to Lord Rama. Let us shun bad action and jealousy.

सदा रामनामे वदा पुर्णकामें।<br>
कदा बाधिजेना ऽऽ पदा नित्य नेमें॥<br>
मदालस्य हा सर्व सोडोनि द्यावा।<br>
प्रभाते मनी राम चिंतीत जावा॥७०॥

**Verse 70**: Let us always meditate on Lord Rama. Let us rise above the sensual pleasures. He who implements this concept is not affected by wordly troubles. Let us not be lazy.

## ७१ - ८०

जयाचेनि नामें महादोष जाती।<br>
जयाचेनि नामें गती पाविजेती॥<br>
जयाचेनि नामें घडे पुण्यठेवा।<br>
प्रभाते मनी राम चिंतीत जावा॥७१॥

**Verse 71**: Lord Rama's Name can set right many deficiencies of our mind. We can follow the noble path if we remember Lord Rama. Remembering Lord Rama creates huge stock of merit for us.

न वेचे कदा ग्रंथचि अर्थ काही।<br>
मुखे नाम उच्चारितां कष्ट नाहीं॥<br>
महाघोर संसारशत्रु जिणावा।<br>
प्रभाते मनी राम चिंतीत जावा॥७२॥

**Verse 72**: If we cannot understand books then we should atleast remember Lord Rama in our mind. What efforts are required for that? Don't entangle your mind in the day to day happenings.

देहेदंडणेचे महादु:ख आहे।<br>
महादु:ख तें नाम घेता न राहे॥<br>
सदाशीव चिंतीतसे देवदेवा।<br>
प्रभाते मनीं राम चिंतीत जावा॥७३॥

**Verse 73**: He who undertake penance puts his body under stress. He who remembers Lord Rama while doing penance is relieved of the stress. God Shiva always remembers Lord Rama.

बहुतांपरी संकटे साधनांची।<br>
व्रते दान उद्यापने ती धनाची॥<br>
दिनाचा दयाळू मनी आठवावा।<br>
प्रभाते मनी राम चिंतीत जावा॥७४॥

**Verse 74**: There are many obstacles in the path of realisation of God. He who has wealth can perform rituals and give donations. Let us remember the protector of the afflicted.

समस्तामधे सार साचार आहे।<br>
कळेना तरी सर्व शोधुन पाहे॥<br>
जिवा संशयो वाउगा तो त्यजावा।<br>
प्रभाते मनी राम चिंतीत जावा॥७५॥

**Verse 75**: Whatever has been said is having ony one substance - Good Action. You can see it for yourself. Let us remove confusion from our mind.

नव्हे कर्म ना धर्म ना योग कांही।<br>
नव्हे भोग ना त्याग ना सांग पाहीं॥<br>
म्हणे दास विश्वास नामी धरावा।<br>
प्रभाते मनी राम चिंतीत जावा॥७६॥

**Verse 76**: If we are not in a postion to perform good acts, similarly if we cannot perform japas and tapas, but then also we can always remember Lord Rama to start with.

करी काम निष्काम या राघवाचे।<br>
करी रुप स्वरुप सर्वां जिवांचे ॥<br>
करि छंद निर्द्वद्व हे गुण गातां।<br>
हरीकीर्तनी वृत्तिविश्वास होतां॥७७॥

**Verse 77**: Remembering Lord Rama will turn our attachment for sensual pleasures into attachment for Good Work. We will understand that other living creatures are no different from us. All the pairs of opposites that we experience will come to an end. We should sing the stories of Lord Rama. Our mind should have faith in him.

अहो ज्या नरा रामविश्वास नाहीं।<br>
तया पामरा बाधिजे सर्व कांही॥<br>
महाराज तो स्वामि कैवल्यदाता।<br>
वृथा वाहणें देहसंसारचिंता॥७८॥

**Verse 78**: He who does not believe in Lord Rama, everything goes adverse to him. Lord Rama is the controller of all. He can grant salvation from the wordly troubles. Dont be unnecessarily sad about the happenings taking place around you.

मना पावना भावना राघवाची।<br>
धरी अंतरीं सोडिं चिंता भवाची॥<br>
भवाची जिवा मानवा भूलि ठेली।<br>
नसे वस्तुचि धारणा व्यर्थ गेली॥७९॥

**Verse 79**: Attachment to Lord Rama is the origin of all good signs. Let us have attachment for Lord Rama. Let us win over the attachment to the world. We keep on indulging in imaginations. We long for something that is not there and then become sad.

धरा श्रीवरा त्या हरा अंतराते।<br>
तरा दुस्तरा त्या परा सागराते॥<br>
सरा वीसरा त्या भरा दुर्भराते।<br>
करा नीकरा त्या खरा मत्सराते॥८०॥

**Verse 80**: Let Shri Rama dwell in our mind. Let us sail smoothly through the ocean of wordly troubles. Let us destroy jealousy in our mind.

## ८१ - ९०

मना मत्सरे नाम सांडूं नको हो।<br>
अती आदरे हा निजध्यास राहो॥<br>
समस्तांमधे नाम हे सार आहे।<br>
दुजी तूळणा तूळितांही न साहे॥८१॥

**Verse 81**: Dont cease to remember Lord Rama under any guise. Let us always understand our true self. The gist of the Vedas and Puranas is that one should remember Lord Rama. Nothing is comparable to this.

बहु नाम या रामनामी तुळेना।<br>
अभाग्या नरा पामरा हे कळेंना॥<br>
विषा औषधा घेतले पार्वतीशे।<br>
जिवा मानवा किंकरा कोण पुसे॥८२॥

**Verse 82**: If we remember others then we wont get the same satisfaction. But people dont understand this. God Shiva, who is the killer of cupid (kama) remembers Lord Rama as a medicine for the poison that he has taken. We are absolutely nothing as compared to God Shiva.

जेणे जाळिला काम तो राम ध्यातो।<br>
उमेसी अती आदरें गूण गातो॥<br>
बहु ज्ञान वैराग्य सामर्थ्य जेथें।<br>
परी अंतरी नामविश्वास तेथें॥८३॥

**Verse 83**: God Shiva who has killed cupid always dwells his mind on Lord Rama. He respectfully tells stories of Lord Rama to Goddess Parvati. God Shiva remembers Lord Rama after having all knowledge, detachment and power.

विठोने शिरी वाहिला देवराणा।<br>
तया अंतरी ध्यास रे त्यासि नेणा॥<br>
निवाला स्वये तापसी चंद्रमौळी।<br>
जिवा सोडवी राम हा अंतकाळीं॥८४॥

**Verse 84**: Vitthal holds God Shankar on his head. God Shankar dwells his mind on Lord Rama. Therefore we should not forget Lord Rama. Who else can save us at the time of death?

भजा राम विश्राम योगेश्वरांचा।<br>
जपू नेमिला नेम गौरीहराचा॥<br>
स्वये नीववी तापसी चंद्रमौळी।<br>
तुम्हां सोडवी राम हा अंतकाळीं॥८५॥

**Verse 85**: Those who strive for salvation find their solace in Lord Rama. Goddess Gouri and God Har always remember him. Let us also remember him.

मुखी राम विश्राम तेथेचि आहे।<br>
सदानंद आनंद सेवोनि आहे॥<br>
तयावीण तो शीण संदेहकारी।<br>
निजधाम हे नाम शोकापहारी॥८६॥

**Verse 86**: He who remembers Lord Rama becomes peaceful. Come and experience this peace. If we dont do this then we will get tired. Lord Rama is our abode and will relieve us of our sorrows.

मुखी राम त्या काम बाधुं शकेना।<br>
गुणे इष्ट धारिष्ट त्याचे चुकेना॥<br>
हरीभक्त तो शक्त कामास भारी।<br>
जगीं धन्य तो मारुती ब्रह्मचारी॥८७॥

**Verse 87**: He who remembers Lord Rama cannot be selfish, he sticks to virtues and is patient. The devotees of Lord Rama can kill cupid. This is how Hanuman has proved his worth.

बहू चांगले नाम या राघवाचे।<br>
अती साजिरे स्वल्प सोपे फुकाचे॥<br>
करी मूळ निर्मूळ घेता भवाचे।<br>
जिवां मानवां हेंचि कैवल्य साचें॥८८॥

**Verse 88**: Remembering Lord Rama is wonderful. It is easy and free. The attachment to the world comes to an end on remembering Lord Rama. Then what remains is the bliss.

जनीं भोजनी नाम वाचे वदावें।<br>
अती आदरे गद्यघोषे म्हणावे॥<br>
हरीचिंतने अन्न सेवीत जावे।<br>
तरी श्रीहरी पाविजेतो स्वभावें॥८९॥

**Verse 89**: Let us remember Lord Rama while we are amidst people, while we take food. While doing so we should have full respect and apply all our resources for that purpose. Let us not eat for the sake of feeding stomach. We should eat with the purpose of having ability to undertake good work.

न ये राम वाणी तया थोर हाणी।<br>
जनीं व्यर्थ प्राणी तया नाम कोणी॥<br>
हरीनाम हें वेदशास्त्रीं पुराणीं।<br>
बहू आगळे बोलिली व्यासवाणी॥९०॥

**Verse 90**: He who does not remember Lord Rama is wasted.' How to remember Rama? ' is the subject matter of Vedas, Shastras and Puranas. This is what has been stated by Maharishi Vyas.

## ९१ - १००

नको वीट मानूं रघुनायकाचा।<br>
अती आदरे बोलिजे राम वाचा॥<br>
न वेंचे मुखी सांपडे रे फुकाचा।<br>
करीं घोष त्या जानकीवल्लभाचा॥९१॥<br>

**Verse 91**: Dont get fed up of Shri Rama. We should remember him with full respect. There is no great effort required to remember Lord Rama. We should always remember the spouse of Sita.

अती आदरें सर्वही नामघोषे।<br>
गिरीकंदरी जाइजे दूरि दोषें॥<br>
हरी तिष्ठतू तोषला नामघोषें।<br>
विशेषें हरामानसीं रामपीसें॥९२॥<br>

**Verse 92**: He who remembers Rama with full devotion drives away all impurities from his mind. Lord Rama likes to stay in the mind of all who remember him. God Shiva fondly remembers him.

जगीं पाहतां देव हा अन्नदाता।<br>
तया लागली तत्त्वता सार चिंता॥<br>
तयाचे मुखी नाम घेता फुकाचे।<br>
मना सांग पां रे तुझे काय वेंचे॥९३॥<br>

**Verse 93**: God takes care of this world. He is worried about the world. What it takes of you to remember him.

तिन्ही लोक जाळुं शके कोप येतां।<br>
निवाला हरु तो मुखे नाम घेतां॥<br>
जपे आदरें पार्वती विश्वमाता।<br>
म्हणोनी म्हणा तेंचि हे नाम आतां॥९४॥<br>

**Verse 94**: God Shankar on becoming angry can reduce to ashes all the three worlds. He is peaceful due to remembering Lord Rama. Goddess Parvati also remembers Lord Rama. Thatswhy we should also remember him.

अजामेळ पापी वदे पुत्रकामे।<br>
तया मुक्ति नारायणाचेनि नामें॥<br>
शुकाकारणे कुंटणी राम वाणी।<br>
मुखें बोलतां ख्याति जाली पुराणीं॥९५॥<br>

**Verse 95**: A sinner Ajamila has his son's name as Rama. Therefore he remembers Lord Rama while calling his son. Due to this he has attained salvation. A prostitute has her parrot's name as Rama. Therefore she remembers Lord Rama. She has made a place for herself in Puranas due to this.

महाभक्त प्रल्हाद हा दैत्यकूळीं।<br>
जपे रामनामावळी नित्यकाळीं॥<br>
पिता पापरुपी तया देखवेना।<br>
जनी दैत्य तो नाम मुखे म्हणेना॥९६॥<br>

**Verse 96**: Pralhada, a great devotee of Lord Rama was born in the house of demons. He always remembered Rama. His Father who was sinful could not like this. He (father) did not remember Lord Rama as he was a demon.

मुखी नाम नाहीं तया मुक्ति कैंची।<br>
अहंतागुणे यातना ते फुकाची॥<br>
पुढे अंत येईल तो दैन्यवाणा।<br>
म्हणोनि म्हणा रे म्हणा देवराणा॥९७॥<br>

**Verse 97**: He who cannot remember cannot find salvation. Such people raise their ego and undergo all troubles. Later they come to an unceremonious end. Therefore we should remember Shri Ram.

हरीनाम नेमस्त पाषाण तारी।<br>
बहु तारीले मानवी देहधारी॥<br>
तया रामनामीं सदा जो विकल्पी।<br>
वदेना कदा जीव तो पापरूपी॥९८॥<br>

**Verse 98**: Lord Rama made rocks float in the sea. He has granted salvation to many human beings. But there are some sinful people who are always confused. Such people cannot remember Ram.

जगीं धन्य वाराणसी पुण्यराशी।<br>
तयेमाजि आतां गतीं पूर्वजांसी॥<br>
मुखे रामनामावळी नित्य काळीं।<br>
जिवा हित सांगे सदा चंद्रमौळी॥९९॥<br>

**Verse 99**: In this world the most holy place is Kashi. He who dies in Kashi attains to salvation alongwith his ancestors. This is possible due to Lord Ram always being remembered in Kashi. God Shankar himself explains the benefit of remembering Rama. Such is its significance.

यथासांग रे कर्म तेंहि घडेना।<br>
घडे धर्म तें पुण्य गांठी पडेना॥<br>
दया पाहतां सर्व भुतीं असेना।<br>
फुकाचे मुखी नाम तेंही वसेना॥१००॥<br>

**Verse 100**: People do not maintain purity in their actions. They do not acquire any merit through their actions. They dont have any love for all. They do not remember Lord Rama.

## १०१ - ११०

जया नावडे नाम त्या यम जाची।<br>
विकल्पे उठे तर्क त्या नर्क ची ची॥<br>
म्हणोनि अती आदरे नाम घ्यावे।<br>
मुखे बोलतां दोष जाती स्वभावें॥१०१॥

**Verse 101**: He who does not remember Lord Rama goes to hell. All sorts of logic that start with confusion end up with hell. Therefore we should respectfully remember Lord Rama. This results in purification of our mind.

अती लीनता सर्वभावे स्वभावें।<br>
जना सज्जनालागिं संतोषवावे॥<br>
देहे कारणीं सर्व लावीत जावें।<br>
सगूणीं अती आदरेसी भजावें॥१०२॥

**Verse 102**: One should be exceedingly polite. One should work for good cause. One should respectfully abide by the good qualities.

हरीकीर्तनीं प्रीति रामीं धरावी।<br>
देहेबुद्धि नीरूपणीं वीसरावी॥<br>
परद्रव्य आणीक कांता परावी।<br>
यदर्थीं मना सांडि जीवीं करावी॥१०३॥

**Verse 103**: Let us always remember Lord Rama. Let us have love at his feet. Let us not have any ego. Let us never long for other's wealth and wife.

क्रियेवीण नानापरी बोलिजेंते।<br>
परी चित्त दुश्चीत तें लाजवीतें॥<br>
मना कल्पना धीट सैराट धांवे।<br>
तया मानवा देव कैसेनि पावे॥१०४॥

**Verse 104**: All said and nothing done. Such behaviour brings shame to our mind. If we think anything we like then we are not going to realise God.

विवेके क्रिया आपुली पालटावी।<br>
अती आदरे शुद्ध क्रीया धरावी॥<br>
जनीं बोलण्यासारिखे चाल बापा।<br>
मना कल्पना सोडिं संसारतापा॥१०५॥

**Verse 105**: Let the conscience prevail over our mind and guide our actions. Let our actions be pure. Let us act on what we say. Let us not burn in the flames of wordly worries.

बरी स्नानसंध्या करी एकनिष्ठा।<br>
विवेके मना आवरी स्थानभ्रष्टा॥<br>
दया सर्वभुतीं जया मानवाला।<br>
सदा प्रेमळू भक्तिभावे निवाला॥१०६॥

**Verse 106**: It is nice to perform the daily rituals. Let the conscience tame our mind when our mind derails from the track. Let us have pity and affection for all. Let us be full of devotion, love and peace.

मना कोपआरोपणा ते नसावी।<br>
मना बुद्धि हे साधुसंगी वसावी॥<br>
मना नष्ट चांडाळ तो संग त्यागीं।<br>
मना होइ रे मोक्षभागी विभागी॥१०७॥

**Verse 107**: Let us shun anger and desire. Let us purify our intellect in association with good people. Let us not be associated with useless and evil people. This is how one can attain salvation.

मना सर्वदा सज्जनाचेनि योगें।<br>
क्रिया पालटे भक्तिभावार्थ लागे॥<br>
क्रियेवीण वाचाळता ते निवारी।<br>
तुटे वाद संवाद तो हीतकारी॥१०८॥

**Verse 108**: We can purfy our actions and work with devotion only when we work in the association of good people. If we do not have the courage to act then we should not talk. Let us shun futile debates and make fruitful disussions.

जनीं वादवेवाद सोडूनि द्यावा।<br>
जनीं वादसंवाद सूखे करावा॥<br>
जगीं तोचि तो शोकसंतापहारी।<br>
तुटे वाद संवाद तो हीतकारी॥१०९॥

**Verse 109**: Let us not indulge in futile debates. Let us spread happiness through meaningful discussions. Let us shun sadness and irritation. Let us shun futile debates and make fruitful disussions.

तुटे वाद संवाद त्याते म्हणावें।<br>
विवेके अहंभाव यातें जिणावें॥<br>
अहंतागुणे वाद नाना विकारी।<br>
तुटे वाद संवाद तो हीतकारी॥११०॥

**Verse 110**: A good disussion is one which puts an end to a debate. Let the conscience overpower the ego. Due to ego we indulge in debates and then lose balance of our mind.

## १११ - १२०

हिताकारणे बोलणे सत्य आहे।<br>
हिताकारणे सर्व शोधुनि पाहें॥<br>
हितकारणे बंड पाखांड वारी।<br>
तुटे वाद संवाद तो हीतकारी॥१११॥

**Verse 111**: We should speak the truth for the sake of benefit. We should understand the whole matter for the sake of benefit. We should not indulge in petty quarrels for the sake of benefit.

जनीं सांगतां ऐकता जन्म गेला।<br>
परी वादवेवाद तैसाचि ठेला॥<br>
उठे संशयो वाद हा दंभधारी।<br>
तुटे वाद संवाद तो हीतकारी॥११२॥

**Verse 112**: The whole life is gone in telling others and in listeneing to others. But petty quarrels are still outsanding. When we get confused while arguing then we should understand that we are wrong.

जनी हीत पंडीत सांडीत गेले।<br>
अहंतागुणे ब्रह्मराक्षस जाले॥<br>
तयाहून व्युत्पन्न तो कोण आहे।<br>
मना सर्व जाणीव सांडुनि राहे॥११३॥

**Verse 113**: The learned people work against their interest when they work under the influence of ego. It is very difficult to find more learned people than who are referred to earlier. Therefore let us shun the ego.

फुकाचे मुखी बोलतां काय वेचे।<br>
दिसंदीस अभ्यंतरी गर्व सांचे॥<br>
क्रियेवीण वाचाळता व्यर्थ आहे।<br>
विचारे तुझा तूंचि शोधुनि पाहे॥११४॥

**Verse 114**: Of what use are the blank words? Due to this in the course of time our ego rises. There is no meaning to our words if we cannot perform. He who thinks over it will understand quickly.

तुटे वाद संवाद तेथें करावा।<br>
विविके अहंभाव हा पालटावा॥<br>
जनीं बोलण्यासारिखे आचरावें।<br>
क्रियापालटे भक्तिपंथेचि जावे॥११५॥

**Verse 115**: We should make discussions to end the ongoing debates. We should change our egoistic attitude under the influence of conscience. Whatever we tell others to do, let us do first. Let us change our course of action and follow the path of devotion.

बहू शापिता कष्टला अंबऋषी।<br>
तयाचे स्वये श्रीहरी जन्म सोशी॥<br>
दिला क्षीरसिंधु तया ऊपमानी।<br>
नुपेक्षी कदा देव भक्ताभिमानी॥११६॥

**Verse 116**: Lord Rama has suffered the pangs of transmigration for the sake of his devotee, Ambarishi. Lord Rama has given Upamanyu, a devotee, a place in his abode. Lord Rama never neglects his devotees.

धुरू लेकरु बापुडे दैन्यवाणे।<br>
कृपा भाकितां दीधली भेटी जेणे॥<br>
चिरंजीव तारांगणी प्रेमखाणी।<br>
नुपेक्षी कदा देव भक्ताभिमानी॥११७॥

**Verse 117**: Dhruva child was five years of age. The Lord immediately attended to his call and granted him a permanant postion in the space as a North Star.

गजेंदु महासंकटी वास पाहे।<br>
तयाकारणे श्रीहरी धांवताहे॥<br>
उडी घातली जाहला जीवदानी।<br>
नुपेक्षी कदा देव भक्ताभिमानी॥११८॥

**Verse 118**: Lord Rama hurried to help out an elephant whose leg was caught by a crocodile in water. The reason was a call given by the elephant to the Lord.

अजामेळ पापी तया अंत आला।<br>
कृपाळूपणे तो जनीं मुक्त केला॥<br>
अनाथासि आधार हा चक्रपाणी।<br>
नुपेक्षी कदा देव भक्ताभिमानी॥११९॥

**Verse 119**: The sinner Ajamila at the time of his death remembered Lord Rama. The reason being his child was also having Rama as his name. The merciful Lord granted salvation to him. Lord Rama supports everyone who does not have any support.

विधीकारणे जाहला मत्स्य वेगीं।<br>
धरी कूर्मरुपे धरा पृष्ठभागी।<br>
जना रक्षणाकारणे नीच योनी।<br>
नुपेक्षी कदा देव भक्ताभिमानी॥१२०॥

**Verse 120**: For the sake of protection of earth he took the form of a fish, a tortoise at some points of time. He does not mind taking a form of a creature of low breed for the good of people at large.

## १२१ - १३०

महाभक्त प्रल्हाद हा कष्टवीला।<br>
म्हणोनी तयाकारणे सिंह जाला॥<br>
न ये ज्वाळ वीशाळ संनधि कोणी।<br>
नुपेक्षी कदा देव भक्ताभिमानी॥१२१॥

**Verse 121**: Lord Rama acquired a form of a Lion God ( Narsimha) for protecting his devotee (Pralhada) .

कृपा भाकिता जाहला वज्रपाणी।<br>
तया कारणें वामनू चक्रपाणी॥<br>
द्विजांकारणे भार्गवू चापपाणी।<br>
नुपेक्षी कदा देव भक्ताभिमानी॥१२२॥

**Verse 122**: He became Waman for the sake of Indra. He descended to earth as Parshurama for the protection of the intellectual class.

अहल्येसतीलागी आरण्यपंथे।<br>
कुडावा पुढे देव बंदी तयांते॥<br>
बळे सोडितां घाव घालीं निशाणी।<br>
नुपेक्षी कदा राम दासाभिमानी॥१२३॥

**Verse 123**: Lord Rama set free Ahilya who became a rock following a curse of her husband. Lord Rama also freed Gods from the shackles of Ravana.

तये द्रौपदीकारणे लागवेगे।<br>
त्वरे धांवतो सर्व सांडूनि मागें॥<br>
कळीलागिं जाला असे बौद्ध मौनी।<br>
नुपेक्षी कदा देव भक्ताभिमानी॥१२४॥

**Verse 124**: The Lord rushed for help leaving aside all matters when a call was made to him by Droupadi. The Lord has descended as Goutam Buddha for the benefit of people in the Kali Age.

अनाथां दिनांकारणे जन्मताहे।<br>
कलंकी पुढे देव होणार आहे॥<br>
तया वर्णिता शीणली वेदवाणी।<br>
नुपेक्षी कदा देव भक्ताभिमानी॥१२५॥

**Verse 125**: Lord Rama takes birth on this earth for the protection of the afflicted. Vedas are unable to comprehend him.

जनांकारणे देव लीलावतारी।<br>
बहुतांपरी आदरें वेषधारी॥<br>
तया नेणती ते जन पापरूपी।<br>
दुरात्मे महानष्ट चांडाळ पापी॥१२६॥

**Verse 126**: Lord Rama descends to this earth in many forms for the sake of the good of the people at large. People who do not understand him are sinners, evil and gone cases.

जगीं धन्य तो रामसूखें निवाला।<br>
कथा ऐकतां सर्व तल्लीन जाला॥<br>
देहेभावना रामबोधे उडाली।<br>
मनोवासना रामरूपीं बुडाली॥१२७॥

**Verse 127**: He is worth living who experiences the bliss of Lord Rama's existence. He is worth living who listens to the stories of Lord Rama. He is worth living who has raised his concious from the level of his body to reach the level of Lord Rama. He is worth living who has merged all his desires in Lord Rama.

मना वासना वासुदेवीं वसों दे।<br>
मना कामना कामसंगी नसो दे॥<br>
मना कल्पना वाउगी ते न कीजे।<br>
मना सज्जना सज्जनी वस्ति कीजे॥१२८॥

**Verse 128**: Let our mind dwell on Lord Rama. Let us shun selfishness. Let us not indulge in evil designs. Let us honestly remain in the association of the honest people.

गतीकारणे संगती सज्जनाची।<br>
मती पालटे सूमती दुर्जनाची॥<br>
रतीनायिकेचा पती नष्ट आहे।<br>
म्हणोनी मनाऽतीत होवोनि राहे॥१२९॥

**Verse 129**: Let us remain in the company of honest people for the sake of better tommorrow. Good thoughts can trasform an evil person. He who always likes sensual pleasures is a gone case. Let us learn to rise above the temperament of the moment.

मना अल्प संकल्प तोही नसावा।<br>
सदा सत्यसंकल्प चित्तीं वसावा॥<br>
जनीं जल्प वीकल्प तोही त्यजावा।<br>
रमाकांत एकान्तकाळी भजावा॥१३०॥

**Verse 130**: Dont plan rubbish. Let the mind always dwell on the truth. Let us stop babbling amidst people. Let us hold Lord Rama in our mind always.

## १३१ - १४०

भजाया जनीं पाहतां राम एकू।<br>
करी बाण एकू मुखी शब्द एकू॥<br>
क्रिया पाहतां उद्धरे सर्व लोकू।<br>
धरा जानकीनायकाचा विवेकू॥१३१॥<br>

**Verse 131**: Lord Rama does what he says. He works for the emancipation of the whole world. Let us understand and praise Lord Rama. Let us hold the hero of Sita at the level of our conscience.

विचारूनि बोले विवंचूनि चाले।<br>
तयाचेनि संतप्त तेही निवाले॥<br>
बरें शोधल्यावीण बोलो नको हो।<br>
जनी चालणे शुद्ध नेमस्त राहो॥१३२॥<br>

**Verse 132**: Lord Rama acts after due meditation. He who has an agitated mind becomes calm after remembering Lord Rama. Let us not speak without knowing the truth. Let our actions amidst people be pure and proportionate.

हरीभक्त वीरक्त विज्ञानराशी।<br>
जेणे मानसी स्थापिलें निश्चयासी॥<br>
तया दर्शने स्पर्शने पुण्य जोडे।<br>
तया भाषणें नष्ट संदेह मोडे॥१३३॥<br>

**Verse 133**: The devotee of Lord Rama is detached from the world, he understands the truth and has a strong determination. Our stock of merit rise if we see such devotee. If we talk with him then our confusion ends.

नसे गर्व आंगी सदा वीतरागी।<br>
क्षमा शांति भोगी दयादक्ष योगी॥<br>
नसे लोभ ना क्षोम ना दैन्यवाणा।<br>
इहीं लक्षणी जाणिजे योगिराणा॥१३४॥<br>

**Verse 134**: A true devotee is free from greed, passion and misery.

धरीं रे मना संगती सज्जनाची।<br>
जेणें वृत्ति हे पालटे दुर्जनाची॥<br>
बळे भाव सद्बुद्धि सन्मार्ग लागे।<br>
महाक्रुर तो काळ विक्राळ भंगे॥१३५॥<br>

**Verse 135**: Let us have association with good people. This will render change in our way of thinking. This will purify our mind and intellect. This will help us attain salvation and win over the death.

भयें व्यापिले सर्व ब्रह्मांड आहे।<br>
भयातीत तें संत आनंत पाहे॥<br>
जया पाहतां द्वैत कांही दिसेना।<br>
भयो मानसीं सर्वथाही असेना॥१३६॥<br>

**Verse 136**: Everyone in this world is shivering with fear. The good people who can see the truth do not have fear. Let us find him who will end our state of separation. Let us find him who will remove fear from our mind.

जिवां श्रेष्ठ ते स्पष्ट सांगोनि गेले।<br>
परी जीव अज्ञान तैसेचि ठेले॥<br>
देहेबुद्धिचें कर्म खोटें टळेना।<br>
जुने ठेवणें मीपणें आकळेना॥१३७॥<br>

**Verse 137**: The great people have told the truth in very clear terms. Still we cannot understand. We cannot raise our conscious beyond our body. We cannot see the truth due to the ego we have.

भ्रमे नाढळे वित्त तें गुप्त जाले।<br>
जिवा जन्मदारिद्र्य ठाकुनि आले॥<br>
देहेबुद्धिचा निश्चयो ज्या टळेना।<br>
जुने ठेवणे मीपणे आकळेना॥१३८॥<br>

**Verse 138**: We have lost the treasure due to our delusion. Now we are rendered poor. He who cannot raise his conscious beyond his body cannot see the truth.

पुढें पाहता सर्वही कोंदलेसें।<br>
अभाग्यास हें दृश्य पाषाण भासे॥<br>
अभावे कदा पुण्य गांठी पडेना।<br>
जुने ठेवणे मीपणे आकळेना॥१३९॥<br>

**Verse 139**: We cannot see the wealth hidden all around. We cannot see the truth. If we do not have faith then we cannot earn any merit.

जयाचे तया चूकले प्राप्त नाहीं।<br>
गुणे गोविले जाहले दुःख देहीं ॥<br>
गुणावेगळी वृत्ति तेहि वळेना।<br>
जुने ठेवणे मीपणे आकळेना॥१४०॥<br>

**Verse 140**: We are not able to get back to the state we have lost. We are entangled here and there. Our mind is not capable of getting out of delusion.

## १४१ - १५०

म्हणे दास सायास त्याचे करावे।<br>
जनीं जाणता पाय त्याचे धरावे॥<br>
गुरू अंजनेवीण तें आकळेना।<br>
जुने ठेवणे मीपणे ते कळेना॥१४१॥<br>

**Verse 141**: We should offer us to the service of the wise person who knows the truth. We should grasp his feet. We cannot see unless an enlightened person guides us.

कळेना कळेना कळेना कळेना।<br>
ढळे नाढळे संशयोही ढळेना॥<br>
गळेना गळेना अहंता गळेना।<br>
बळें आकळेना मिळेना मिळेना॥१४२॥<br>

**Verse 142**: We are not capable of understanding the truth. We are not capable of experiencing the truth. We are not capable of dispelling confusion from our mind. We are not capable to renounce our ego. It appears that it is beyond our power to understand Lord Rama.

अविद्यागुणे मानवा उमजेना।<br>
भ्रमे चुकले हीत ते आकळेना॥<br>
परीक्षेविणे बांधले दृढ नाणें।<br>
परी सत्य मिथ्या असें कोण जाणें॥१४३॥<br>

**Verse 143**: We cannot understand due to delusion. We are confused and unable to decide our interest. We have accepted the currency notes without verifying the validity.

जगी पाहतां साच ते काय आहे।<br>
अती आदरे सत्य शोधुन पाहे॥<br>
पुढे पाहतां पाहतां देव जोडे।<br>
भ्रम भ्रांति अज्ञान हें सर्व मोडे॥१४४॥<br>

**Verse 144**: What is the truth? Let us explore in the world. Let us find out with the full devotion of mind. If we move in this manner then we will realise God. This way we will do away with all our ignorance and confusion.

सदा वीषयो चिंतितां जीव जाला।<br>
अहंभाव अज्ञान जन्मास आला॥<br>
विवेके सदा स्वस्वरुपी भरावे।<br>
जिवा ऊगमी जन्म नाही स्वभावें॥१४५॥<br>

**Verse 145**: We are always concerned about the sensual pleasures. Ego and ignorance is rising in our mind. Let us tame our mind with conscience. Let us understand our true self.

दिसे लोचनी ते नसे कल्पकोडी।<br>
अकस्मात आकारले काळ मोडी॥<br>
पुढे सर्व जाईल कांही न राहे।<br>
मना संत आनंत शोधुनि पाहे॥१४६॥<br>

**Verse 146**: What appears to our eyes is not the truth. We are nursing our body with great care but death may destroy it any moment. Let us try to find out the infinite and all-pervading truth.

फुटेना तुटेना चळेना ढळेना।<br>
सदा संचले मीपणे ते कळेना॥<br>
तया एकरूपासि दूजे न साहे।<br>
मना संत आनंत शोधुनि पाहें॥१४७॥<br>

**Verse 147**: That element which cannot break or move is there. That is there but we stand separated from that due to our ego. Let us drive out this ego from our mind.

निराकार आधार ब्रह्मादिकांचा।<br>
जया सांगतां शीणली वेदवाचा॥<br>
विवेके तदाकार होऊनि राहें।<br>
मना संत आनंत शोधुनि पाहे॥१४८॥<br>

**Verse 148**: This element is formless. It is the support for Brahmas and others. This element cannot be comprehended by Vedas. Let us reach out that element on the basis of conscience.

जगी पाहतां चर्मलक्षी न लक्षे।<br>
जगी पाहता ज्ञानचक्षी निरक्षे॥<br>
जनीं पाहता पाहणे जात आहे।<br>
मना संत आनंत शोधुनि पाहे॥१४९॥<br>

**Verse 149**: We cannot see the all-pervading element with our eyes. We can see it through the eyes of knowledge. When we see that element then we lose our existence.

नसे पीत ना श्वेत ना श्याम कांही।<br>
नसे व्यक्त अव्यक्त ना नीळ नाहीं॥<br>
म्हणे दास विश्वासतां मुक्ति लाहे।<br>
मना संत आनंत शोधुनि पाहे॥१५०॥<br>

**Verse 150**: That element is neither yellow nor white nor black. It niether appears nor has it disappeared. Let us find that element. Let us attain to the state of eternal bliss.

## १५१ - १६०

खरें शोधितां शोधितां शोधिताहे।<br>
मना बोधिता बोधिता बोधिताहे॥<br>
परी सर्वही सज्जनाचेनि योगे।<br>
बरा निश्चयो पाविजे सानुरागे॥१५१॥

**Verse 151**: Let us find the truth. Let our mind experience the truth. All this is possible through association with good people. Let us have a strong determination and let us focus on our purpose.

बहूतांपरी कूसरी तत्त्वझाडा।<br>
परी अंतरी पाहिजे तो निवाडा॥<br>
मना सार साचार ते वेगळे रे।<br>
समस्तांमधे एक ते आगळे रे॥१५२॥

**Verse 152**: We may impress others through effective speaking. But let us understand the truth. The gist of the truth is good action. Let us have courage to act honestly.

नव्हे पिंडज्ञाने नव्हे तत्त्वज्ञाने ।<br>
समाधान कांही नव्हे तानमाने॥<br>
नव्हे योगयागें नव्हे भोगत्यागें।<br>
समाधान ते सज्जनाचेनि योगे॥१५३॥

**Verse 153**: Our mind cannot become stable after learning physics, chemistry, philosophy and other such sciences in isolation. Niether can undertaking penance or renunciation can make it stable. The mind becomes stable only in the association with good people.

महावाक्य तत्त्वादिके पंचकर्णे।<br>
खुणे पाविजे संतसंगे विवर्णे॥<br>
द्वितीयेसि संकेत जो दाविजेतो।<br>
तया सांडुनी चंद्रमा भाविजेतो॥१५४॥

**Verse 154**: The great mantras, the great philosophies, everything we can understand in association with the good people. They tell us their experience and guide our way.

दिसेना जनी तेचि शोधुनि पाहे।<br>
बरे पाहता गूज तेथेचि आहे॥<br>
करी घेउ जाता कदा आढळेना।<br>
जनी सर्व कोंदाटले ते कळेना॥१५५॥

**Verse 155**: Let us try to find out the element that is invisible. There lies the secret. We cannot just hold it exclusively with us. That element is all-pervading.

म्हणे जाणता तो जनी मूर्ख पाहे।<br>
अतर्कासि तर्की असा कोण आहे॥<br>
जनीं मीपणे पाहता पाहवेना।<br>
तया लक्षितां वेगळे राहवेना॥१५६॥

**Verse 156**: He who calls himself a learned person is a fool. The element that we have been talking about is beyond logic. So no one can explain it logically. One who does not have any ego can realise that element. The moment that element is realised, the identity of the person who realises gets merged with that element.

बहू शास्त्र धुंडाळता वाड आहे।<br>
जया निश्चयो येक तोही न साहे॥<br>
मती भांडती शास्त्रबोधे विरोधें।<br>
गती खुंटती ज्ञानबोधे प्रबोधे॥१५७॥

**Verse 157**: There is no benefit in reading lot of books. This may affect our determination. Our mind may go against the Shastras if our mind becomes agumentative after reading a lot. This will hamper our progress of self-realisation.

श्रुती न्याय मीमांसके तर्कशास्त्रे।<br>
स्मृती वेद वेदान्तवाक्ये विचित्रे॥<br>
स्वये शेष मौनावला स्थीर पाहे।<br>
मना सर्व जाणीव सांडून राहे॥१५८॥

**Verse 158**: Shrutis, Laws, Mimansas, Shastras, Smrities, Vedas are very difficult for one to understand. Even the great Shesh who is the seat of God Vishnu shuts his thousand mouths and maintains silence on this matter. Therefore likes of us do not have any scope for ego.

जेणे मक्षिका भक्षिली जाणिवेची।<br>
तया भोजनाची रुची प्राप्त कैची॥<br>
अहंभाव ज्या मानसीचा विरेना।<br>
तया ज्ञान हे अन्न पोटी जिरेना॥१५९॥

**Verse 159**: A person is eating a very delicious food. With the last morcel he eats a fly. All the good taste of the meals is now gone. Similarly one who is egoistic cannot have any knowledge.

नको रे मना वाद हा खेदकारी।<br>
नको रे मना भेद नानाविकारी॥<br>
नको रे मना शीकवूं पूढिलांसी।<br>
अहंभाव जो राहिला तूजपासी॥१६०॥

**Verse 160**: Let us not indulge in debates that make us feel sad. Let us not have the habbit of useless analysis and hair-splitting. What to teach others when we have ego with us.

## १६१ - १७०

अहंतागुणे सर्वही दुःख होते।<br>
मुखे बोलिले ज्ञान ते व्यर्थ जाते॥<br>
सुखी राहता सर्वही सूख आहे।<br>
अहंता तुझी तुंचि शोधुन पाहे॥१६१॥

**Verse 161**: Ego causes all sorrows. Ego renders all knowledge useless. A peaceful mind is the abode of bliss. Let us realise our true self.

अहंतागुणे नीति सांडी विवेकी।<br>
अनीतीबळे श्लाघ्यता सर्व लोकी॥<br>
परी अंतरी अर्वही साक्ष येते।<br>
प्रमाणांतरे बुद्धि सांडूनि जाते॥१६२॥

**Verse 162**: He who is egoist does not abide by ethics. He may earn a name amidst people. But his mind experiences uneasiness. He who has ego does not accept any evidence against his opinion.

देहेबुद्धिचा निश्चयो दृढ जाला।<br>
देहातीत ते हीत सांडीत गेला॥<br>
देहेबुद्धि ते आत्मबुद्धि करावी।<br>
सदा संगती सज्जनाची धरावी॥१६३॥

**Verse 163**: He who is entangled in sensual pleasures loses his larger interest. Our conscious should not be at the level of our body. It should be at the level of truth.

मनें कल्पिला वीषयो सोडवावा।<br>
मनें देव निर्गूण तो ओळखावा॥<br>
मनें कल्पिता कल्पना ते सरावी।<br>
सदा संगती सज्जनाची धरावी॥१६४॥

**Verse 164**: Let us free our mind from the tangle of sensual pleasures. Let us understand the all-pervading God. Let us shun false imaginations. Let us have assciation with good people.

देहादीक प्रपंच हा चिंतियेला।<br>
परी अंतरी लोभ निश्चित ठेला॥<br>
हरीचिंतने मुक्तिकांता करावी।<br>
सदा संगती सज्जनांची धरावी॥१६५॥

**Verse 165**: One who indulges in sensual pleasures keeps his mind after senses. Let us attain salvation by dwelling our mind on Shri Hari.

अहंकार विस्तारला या देहाचा।<br>
स्त्रियापुत्रमित्रादिके मोह त्यांचा॥<br>
बळे भ्रांति हें जन्मचिंता हरावी।<br>
सदा संगती सज्जनाची धरावी॥१६६॥

**Verse 166**: In the begining one is concerned about himself. Later he is concerned about wife and children. Let us come out of such worries. Let us have association with good people.

बरा निश्चयो शाश्वताचा करावा।<br>
म्हणे दास संदेह तो वीसरावा॥<br>
घडीने घडी सार्थकाची धरावी।<br>
सदा संगती सज्जनाची धरावी॥१६७॥

**Verse 167**: Let our determination rest on the ever-lasting spirit. Let us stop getting confused. Let us put every moment of our life to good use.

करी वृत्ती जो संत तो संत जाणा।<br>
दुराशागुणे जो नव्हे दैन्यवाणा॥<br>
उपाधी देहेबुद्धीते वाढवीते।<br>
परी सज्जना केविं बाधु शके ते॥१६८॥

**Verse 168**: He is good who is good at heart and who does not yield to bad ideas. If we praise someone then his ego rises. If we praise a good person he is not affected.

नसे अंत आनंत संता पुसावा।<br>
अहंकारविस्तार हा नीरसावा॥<br>
गुणेवीण निर्गुण तो आठवावा।<br>
देहेबुद्धिचा आठवु नाठवावा॥१६९॥

**Verse 169**: Let us take refuge at the feet of a good preceptor for knowing the infinite spirit. Let us not expand our ego. Let us dwell on the eternal spirit in our mind. Let us not go after sensual pleasures.

देहेबुद्धि हे ज्ञानबोधे त्यजावी।<br>
विवेके तये वस्तुची भेटी घ्यावी॥<br>
तदाकार हे वृत्ति नाही स्वभावे।<br>
म्हणोनी सदा तेचि शोधीत जावे॥१७०॥

**Verse 170**: Let us have the knowledge that will end our attachment to sensual pleasures. Let us deal with everything with conscience. The mind becomes restless when it dwells on the sensual pleasures. Let us understand our true self.

## १७१ - १८०

असे सार साचार तें चोरलेसे।<br>
इहीं लोचनी पाहता दृश्य भासे॥<br>
निराभास निर्गुण तें आकळेना।<br>
अहंतागुणे कल्पिताही कळेना॥१७१॥

**Verse 171**: If we do not undertake the good work then we cannot realise the eternal spirit. We cannot look at the eternal spirit with our eyes. One can never realise the eternal spirit who has ego in mind.

स्फुरे वीषयी कल्पना ते अविद्या।<br>
स्फुरे ब्रह्म रे जाण माया सुविद्या॥<br>
मुळीं कल्पना दो रुपें तेचि जाली।<br>
विवेके तरी स्वस्वरुपी मिळाली॥१७२॥

**Verse 172**: He who goes after the sensual pleasures is ignorant. He who has knowledge realises the eternal spirit. Let us try to realise our true self.

स्वरुपी उदेला अहंकार राहो।<br>
तेणे सर्व आच्छादिले व्योम पाहो॥<br>
दिशा पाहतां ते निशा वाढताहे।<br>
विवेके विचारे विवंचुनि पाहे॥१७३॥

**Verse 173**: Ego is what has separated us from the eternal spirit. Ego is the cause for craving for sensual pleasures. Let us think on this point.

जया चक्षुने लक्षिता लक्षवेना।<br>
भवा भक्षिता रक्षिता रक्षवेना॥<br>
क्षयातीत तो अक्षयी मोक्ष देतो।<br>
दयादक्ष तो साक्षिने पक्ष घेतो॥१७४॥

**Verse 174**: The truth cannot be seen with our eyes. If we run after sensual pleasures then who can protect us? One who is merciful observes whatever happens and is never involved in the happenings.

विधी निर्मिती लीहितो सर्व भाळी।<br>
परी लीहिता कोण त्याचे कपाळी॥<br>
हरू जाळितो लोक संहारकाळी।<br>
परी शेवटी शंकरा कोण जाळी॥१७५॥

**Verse 175**: God Brahma writes destiny of each and every one on their forehead. But who writes the destiny of God Brahma on his forehead. God Har destroys everything at the end. But who is the destination of God Har?

जगी द्वादशादित्य हे रुद्र अक्रा।<br>
असंख्यात संख्या करी कोण शक्रा॥<br>
जगी देव धुंडाळिता आढळेना।<br>
जगी मुख्य तो कोण कैसा कळेना॥१७६॥

**Verse 176**: Similarly there are other Gods who are assigned their responsibilites. Now how should we decide who is the best God?

तुटेना फुटेना कदा देवराणा।<br>
चळेना ढळेना कदा दैन्यवाणा॥<br>
कळेना कळेना कदा लोचनासी।<br>
वसेना दिसेना जगी मीपणासी॥१७७॥

**Verse 177**: The eternal spirit remains in the place forever. The spirit cannot be seen with the eyes.

जया मानला देव तो पुजिताहे।<br>
परी देव शोधुनि कोणी न पाहे॥<br>
जगी पाहता देव कोट्यानुकोटी।<br>
जया मानली भक्ति जे तेचि मोठी॥१७८॥

**Verse 178**: People worship the God whom they like. But they do not try to find out the truth. There are crores of Gods that are worshipped in this world. Everone thinks that his devotion to the God is the best one.

तिन्ही लोक जेथूनि निर्माण झाले।<br>
तया देवरायासि कोणी न बोले॥<br>
जगीं थोरला देव तो चोरलासे।<br>
गुरूवीण तो सर्वथाही न दीसे॥१७९॥

**Verse 179**: Let us try to realise that God who has created all the three worlds. This greatest God is not visible without taking refuge at the feet of the preceptor.

गुरु पाहता पाहता लक्ष कोटी।<br>
बहूसाल मंत्रावळी शक्ति मोठी॥<br>
मनी कामना चेटके धातमाता।<br>
जनी व्यर्थ रे तो नव्हे मुक्तिदाता॥१८०॥

**Verse 180**: There are crores of preceptors in this world. They know many mantras and weld tremendous power. They have desires and cravings in their mind. They are of no use. They cannot take their disciples to the state of salvation.

## १८१ - १८९

नव्हे चेटकी चाळकू द्रव्यभोंदु।<br>
नव्हे निंदकू मत्सरू भक्तिमंदू॥<br>
नव्हे उन्मतू वेसनी संगबाधू।<br>
जनी ज्ञानिया तोचि साधु अगाधू॥१८१॥

**Verse 181**: A preceptor should not indulge in gimmicks. He should not be after money. He should not abuse others. He should not have jealousy for others. He should not be intoxicated with addiction of any sort. He should not be like what we have said earlier. He should have a good and indepth knowledge. He should not indulge in spreading sparks of discontent here and there.

नव्हे वाउगी चाहुटी काम पोटी।<br>
क्रियेवीण वाचाळता तेचि मोठी॥<br>
मुखे बोलिल्यासारिखे चालताहे।<br>
मना सद्गुरु तोचि शोधुनि पाहे॥१८२॥

**Verse 182**: He should not just speak everything and do nothing. He who acts on his words is a good preceptor. We should take refuge at the feet of such preceptor.

जनी भक्त ज्ञानी विवेकी विरागी।<br>
कृपाळु मनस्वी क्षमावंत योगी॥<br>
प्रभु दक्ष व्युत्पन्न चातुर्य जाणे।<br>
तयाचेनि योगे समाधान बाणे॥१८३॥

**Verse 183**: He should have knowledge, conscience and detachment for the world. He should be kind hearted, forgiving and he should control his mind. He should gauge the depth of his disciples. If we take refuge at the feet of such preceptor then we can get satisfaction.

नव्हे तोचि जाले नसे तेचि आले।<br>
कळो लागले सज्जनाचेनि बोले॥<br>
अनिर्वाच्य ते वाच्य वाचे वदावे।<br>
मना संत आनंत शोधीत जावे॥१८४॥

**Verse 184**: Now we are at the feet of a good preceptor. Now we can realise the infinite truth.

लपावे अति आदरे रामरुपी।<br>
भयातीत निश्चीत ये स्वस्वरुपी॥<br>
कदा तो जनी पाहतांही दिसेना।<br>
सदा ऐक्य तो भिन्नभावे वसेना॥१८५॥

**Verse 185**: Let us hide in the abode of Lord Rama. There are no fears and worries over there. The abode of Lord Rama can be experienced though it is not visible to eyes.

सदा सर्वदा राम सन्नीध आहे।<br>
मना सज्जना सत्य शोधुन पाहे॥<br>
अखंडीत भेटी रघूराजयोगू।<br>
मना सांडीं रे मीपणाचा वियोगू॥१८६॥

**Verse 186**: Lord Rama is always standing near us. Let us see the truth. Let us unite with Lord Rama forever. Let us shun ego that keeps us in separation from Lord Rama.

भुते पिंड ब्रह्मांड हे ऐक्य आहे।<br>
परी सर्वही स्वस्वरुपी न साहे॥<br>
मना भासले सर्व काही पहावे।<br>
परी संग सोडुनि सुखी रहावे॥१८७॥

**Verse 187**: All the living beings are actually one with the Supreme Being. But that is not what our identity is. Let us observe whatever comes to our experience. Let us not get involved and be happy.

देहेभान हे ज्ञानशस्त्रे खुडावे।<br>
विदेहीपणे भक्तिमार्गेचि जावे॥<br>
विरक्तीबळे निंद्य सर्वै त्यजावे।<br>
परी संग सोडुनि सुखी रहावे॥१८८॥

**Verse 188**: Let the knowledge remove conscious away from the sensual pleasures. Let us devote to the truth. Let us detach from the world. Let us shun everything condemned in the world. Let us not get involved. Let us observe whatever takes place.

मही निर्मिली देव तो ओळखावा।<br>
जया पाहतां मोक्ष तत्काळ जीवा॥<br>
तया निर्गुणालागी गूणी पहावे।<br>
परी संग सोडुनि सुखे रहावे॥१८९॥

**Verse 189**: Let us understand the God who has created the world. He who sees God becomes blissful. Even when the God is formless still we can see him taking forms. Let us maintain detachment and equanimity.

नव्हे कार्यकर्ता नव्हे सृष्टिभर्ता।<br>
पुरेहून पर्ता न लिंपे विवर्ता॥<br>
तया निर्विकल्पासि कल्पित जावे।<br>
परि संग सोडुनि सुखे रहावे॥१९०॥

**Verse 190**: God is above the creator and the creation. He does not get involved in anything. He is without any form. Let us dwell on him. Let us get detached from the world and let us be happy by maintaining equanimity of mind.

## १९१ - २००

देहेबुद्धिचा निश्चयो ज्या ढळेना।<br>
तया ज्ञान कल्पांतकाळी कळेना॥<br>
परब्रह्म तें मीपणे आकळेना।<br>
मनी शून्य अज्ञान हे मावळेना॥१९१॥

**Verse 191**: He who has conscious at the level of sensual pleasures can never understand the truth. The eternal spirit cannot be experienced by him who has ego. If we think that everything is blank then this is our ignorance.

मना ना कळे ना ढळे रुप ज्याचे।<br>
दुजेवीण तें ध्यान सर्वोत्तमाचे॥<br>
तया खुण ते हीन दृष्टांत पाहे।<br>
तेथे संग निःसंग दोन्ही न साहे॥१९२॥

**Verse 192**: We cannot understand him. He never changes. If we meditate on him then we merge in him. God's form cannot be explained by citing wordly examples.

नव्हे जाणता नेणता देवराणा।<br>
न ये वर्णिता वेदशास्त्रा पुराणा॥<br>
नव्हे दृश्य अदृश्य साक्षी तयाचा।<br>
श्रुती नेणती नेणती अंत त्याचा॥१९३॥

**Verse 193**: He is beyond all consciousness. He cannot be understood. That does not mean that he is not there. He is neither visible nor invisible. He is observing all. When a discussion of God starts in Vedas, Vedas say that he is beyond all discussions.

वसे हृदयी देव तो कोण कैसा।<br>
पुसे आदरे साधकू प्रश्न ऐसा॥<br>
देहे टाकिता देव कोठे पहातो ।<br>
परि मागुता ठाव कोठे रहातो॥१९४॥

**Verse 194**: Where does the God reside in our mind. This is a question asked by a devotee. He wants to know where the God will reside when our body falls. At that time whether god will shift his place.

बसे हृदयी देव तो जाण ऐसा।<br>
नभाचेपरी व्यापकू जाण तैसा॥<br>
सदा संचला येत ना जात कांही।<br>
तयावीण कोठे रिता ठाव नाही॥१९५॥

**Verse 195**: God in our mind is as wide as the sky. He neither comes from anywhere nor goes to anywhere. He is always there. Nothing exists in this world without him.

नभी वावरे जा अणुरेणु काही।<br>
रिता ठाव या राघवेवीण नाही॥<br>
तया पाहता पाहता तोचि जाले।<br>
तेथे लक्ष आलक्ष सर्वे बुडाले॥१९६॥

**Verse 196**: Whatever atoms and molecules are present everywhere there is no space without him. He who looks at him is merged with him. Now there is no scope for any differentiation.

नभासारिखे रुप या राघवाचे।<br>
मनी चिंतिता मूळ तुटे भवाचे॥<br>
तया पाहता देहबुद्धी उरेना।<br>
सदा सर्वदा आर्त पोटी पुरेना॥१९७॥

**Verse 197**: Lord Rama is like the sky. If we remeber him then we get detached from the world. Our conscious rises above the level of sensual pleasures after looking at him. There cannot be any separation from him thereafter.

नभे व्यापिले सर्व सृष्टीस आहे।<br>
रघूनायका ऊपमा ते न साहे॥<br>
दुजेवीण जो तोचि तो हा स्वभावे।<br>
तया व्यापकू व्यर्थ कैसे म्हणावे॥१९८॥

**Verse 198**: The sky covers the universe. Lord Rama is all-pervading spirit. Therefore we cannot compare him with the sky.

अती जीर्ण विस्तीर्ण ते रुप आहे।<br>
तेथे तर्कसंपर्क तोही न साहे॥<br>
अती गुढ ते दृश्य तत्काळ सोपे।<br>
दुजेवीण जे खुण स्वामिप्रतापे॥१९९॥

**Verse 199**: God is always in existence. He is boundless. He cannot be understood through logic. A preceptor can merge us with the God.

कळे आकळे रुप ते ज्ञान होता।<br>
तेथे आटली सर्वसाक्षी अवस्था॥<br>
मना उन्मनी शब्द कुंठीत राहे।<br>
तो रे तोचि तो राम सर्वत्र पाहे॥२००॥

**Verse 200**: He who realises Lord Rama finds him in existence everywhere.

## २०१ - २०५

कदा ओळखीमाजि दूजे दिसेना।<br>
मनी मानसी द्वैत काही वसेना॥<br>
बहूता दिसा आपली भेट जाली।<br>
विदेहीपणे सर्व काया निवाली॥२०१॥

**Verse 201**: Now there is no relevance of 'I' and 'You'. A person who realises Lord Rama sees him everywhere.

मना गुज रे तूज हे प्राप्त झाले।<br>
परी अंतरी पाहिजे यत्न केले॥<br>
सदा श्रवणे पाविजे निश्चयासी।<br>
धरी सज्जनसंगती धन्य होसी॥२०२॥

**Verse 202**: We are told the secret about the God. But we should strive for realisation of God. Let us associate with good people to make our existence worthwhile.

मना सर्वही संग सोडूनि द्यावा।<br>
अती आदरे सज्जनाचा धरावा॥<br>
जयाचेनि संगे महादुःख भंगे।<br>
जनी साधनेवीण सन्मार्ग लागे॥२०३॥

**Verse 203**: Let us have detachment for the world. Let us associate with good people with full honour. Good association will end our sorrows. Good association will keep us on the right track.

मना संग हा सर्वसंगास तोडी।<br>
मना संग हा मोक्ष तात्काळ जोडी॥<br>
मना संग हा साधना शीघ्र सोडी।<br>
मना संग हा द्वैत निःशेष मोडी॥२०४॥

**Verse 204**: Good association results in detachment from the world. Good association leads to salvation.

मनाची शते ऐकता दोष जाती।<br>
मतीमंद ते साधना योग्य होती॥<br>
चढे ज्ञान वैराग्य सामर्थ्य अंगी।<br>
म्हणे दास विश्वासत मुक्ति भोगी॥२०५॥

**Verse 205**: Let us remove impurities of our mind by listening to the verses for mind. Let us transform our conscious that is dull into an alert one. Let us gain knowledge, detachment to the world and ability of mind by listening to the verses for mind. Let us follow the royal path for attaining salvation.

॥ जय जय रघुवीर समर्थ ॥
